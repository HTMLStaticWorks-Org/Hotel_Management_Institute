The 4-Year Trajectory

Your Journey From Novice to Global Hotelier

How HMI structures your academic evolution through intensive hands-on immersion and leadership development.

Year 1 • London Campus

Classical Etiquette & Culinary Foundation

Mastering food preparation, French terminology, restaurant service etiquette, housekeeping hygiene standards, and guest psychology.

Year 2 • Geneva Campus

Front Office Systems & Hotel Operations

PMS reservation management, room revenue algorithms, luxury concierge protocols, and financial accounting for hospitality assets.

Year 3 • Global Placement

6-Month Paid International 5-Star Residency

Direct employment with partners like The Ritz-Carlton Paris, Aman Tokyo, or Burj Al Arab Dubai with dedicated mentor guidance.

Year 4 • Capstone & Leadership

Executive Strategy & Asset Leadership

Hotel feasibility studies, sustainable resort development, luxury brand licensing, and executive job placement recruitment.
