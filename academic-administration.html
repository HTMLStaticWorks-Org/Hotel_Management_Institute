Academic Rigor

Regulations & Bologna ECTS Credit Guidelines

Degrees granted by HMI are structured on the European Credit Transfer and Accumulation System (ECTS), conferring 180 ECTS for Bachelor degrees and 90 ECTS for Master degrees.

Attendance Threshold: 85% Strict Policy

Practical kitchen labs and front-office simulations mandate at least 85% attendance for exam hall admission.

Academic Integrity & Turnitin Verification

All research essays and case analysis capstones undergo automated AI & plagiarism vetting.

Student Academic Support Services

Writing & Research Clinic

One-on-one editorial coaching for thesis manuscripts, business plans, and market feasibility studies.

Hospitality French & German Labs

Targeted language tutorials focusing on kitchen command vocabulary, restaurant service French, and concierge nuances.

Faculty Office Hours & Mentorship

Weekly open advising hours with senior professors and visiting hotel executives.
