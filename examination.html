Conduct Code

Examination Hall Regulations

  • Hall Ticket & Student ID: Mandatory physical printout required for biometric identity verification at hall entrance.
  • Professional Uniform Dress Code: Tailored institutional blazers for written exams; immaculate whites, toque, and steel-toed boots for kitchen practicals.
  • Strict Electronic Prohibition: Smartwatches, smartphones, and unauthorized digital tablets are strictly banned.
