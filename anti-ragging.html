Anti-Ragging & Safeguarding Policy

Zero Tolerance for harassment, intimidation, or hazing. We uphold an uncompromised culture of mutual dignity, equality, and student safety.

Institutional Directive

Strict Zero-Tolerance Mandate Across Campuses

At HMI Geneva & London, ragging in any form—verbal, physical, psychological, or digital—is treated as a grave disciplinary violation punishable by immediate rustication and legal notification.

Statutory Legal Framework

Compliant with the Swiss Federal Penal Code (Art. 180 & 181 on intimidation) and the United Kingdom Protection from Harassment Act 1997.
