Institute Administration & Governance

Committed to the highest standards of institutional stewardship, transparent governance, and student-first service.

Institutional Leadership

Board of Governors & Executive Council

The Institute is governed by an independent Board comprising European hospitality dignitaries, university chancellors, and luxury brand executives.

The Council oversees institutional compliance with Swiss higher education ordinances, curriculum modernization, and philanthropic endowments.

Administrative Transparency Charter

Audited annually by PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC) Geneva under Swiss Federal Education Standards.
