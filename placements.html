Strategic Career Navigation

The 4-Stage Placement Blueprint

A structured, high-touch process ensuring every student aligns their innate talents with the ideal luxury brand.

01
Talent Profiling & Career Diagnostic

Psychometric assessments, leadership style evaluations, and one-on-one sessions with our Career Dean.

02
Executive Dossier & Video Audition

Crafting bespoke hospitality CVs, professional corporate headshots, and video portfolio presentations.

03
Global Recruitment Days (On-Campus)

HR Vice Presidents from 40+ luxury chains visit our Geneva and London campuses for dedicated interview rounds.

04
Offer Negotiation & Visa Sponsorship

Legal and visa assistance ensuring smooth international relocation to properties in Switzerland, UK, UAE, or USA.
