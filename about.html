Four Decades of Swiss & British Hospitality Pedagogy

Bridging the artisanal traditions of European grand hotels with modern management science and global leadership ethics.

Our Origins

Born on the Shores of Lake Geneva in 1984

HMI was founded by an alliance of Swiss grand hoteliers and British hospitality scholars seeking to codify what makes the European grand hotel experience universally revered.

From an initial cohort of 32 students in Montreux, HMI has evolved into a global educational institution with flagship campuses in Geneva and London Mayfair, training over 1,200 future hospitality executives each year.

Our Vision

To Be the Global Compass for Luxury Hospitality Leadership

We envision a hospitality industry led by individuals who champion human connection, aesthetic refinement, and environmental stewardship, ensuring the timeless art of service flourishes in the digital age.

Our Mission

Educate, Cultivate, and Propel Transformative Hospitality Leaders

Through rigorous academic exploration, hands-on operational immersion, and mentorship from Michelin-starred chefs and luxury general managers, we empower our graduates to lead with emotional intelligence and business acumen.
