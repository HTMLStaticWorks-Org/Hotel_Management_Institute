Academic Programs & Specializations

Seven world-class curricula engineered with Swiss precision and British academic rigor, preparing students for executive roles across international hospitality.

Undergraduate Degree Dual Swiss-UK Award

1. B.Sc. International Hotel Management

A premier 3-year honours degree training global hoteliers in financial modeling, yield strategy, executive guest relations, human capital leadership, and asset lifecycle management.

Duration
3 Years (6 Semesters + 6-Mo Residency)
Eligibility
High School Diploma (IB/A-Levels), IELTS 6.0
Core Curriculum
Opera Cloud PMS, Hotel Finance, Luxury Hospitality Marketing, Housekeeping Systems, Sustainable Resort Planning.
Career Opportunities
Resident Manager, Operations Director, Front Office Head, Revenue Strategist.
Apply for Program View Fee Structure
Postgraduate Master Executive Track

2. Master in International Hospitality Leadership

Designed for high-potential professionals and graduates aspiring to command luxury brand portfolios, private member clubs, and luxury real estate hospitality assets.

Duration
18 Months (Includes Global Consulting Project)
Eligibility
Bachelor's Degree in any discipline + Interview
Core Curriculum
Hospitality Asset Valuation, Cross-Cultural Luxury Psychology, Corporate Finance, Digital Guest Transformation, Crisis Governance.
Career Opportunities
Vice President of Hospitality, General Manager, Luxury Brand Ambassador, Asset Portfolio Manager.
Apply for Program View Fee Structure
Grand Diploma Michelin Mentorship

3. Grand Diploma in Haute Cuisine & Patisserie

Comprehensive training in classical French culinary disciplines, modern sous-vide sciences, artisanal bakery, molecular textures, and tasting menu design.

Duration
2 Years (4 Semesters Intensive Lab Work)
Eligibility
High School Certificate & Culinary Aptitude Assessment
Core Curriculum
Sauces & Stocks, Butchery, Sugar Sculpture, Chocolate Tempering, Kitchen Cost Control, Michelin Kitchen Sanitation.
Career Opportunities
Executive Chef, Head Pastry Chef, Culinary Consultant, Private Superyacht Chef.
Apply for Program View Fee Structure
Professional Diploma Court of Master Sommeliers

4. Diploma in Food & Beverage Service & Sommelier Sciences

Mastering the balance of high-touch service, fine dining restaurant management, cellar acquisition, terroir chemistry, and modern mixology.

Duration
1 Year (12 Months + WSET Levels 1-3)
Eligibility
High School Diploma (Min 18 years of age)
Core Curriculum
Table Service Etiquette, Wine Chemistry, Spirits Distillation, Banqueting Profitability, Bar Design & POS Integration.
Career Opportunities
F&B Director, Head Sommelier, Mixology Consultant, Fine Dining Maître d'Hôtel.
Apply for Program View Fee Structure
Certificate & Diploma Les Clefs d'Or Protocols

5. Front Office Management & VIP Concierge Services

Commanding the face of luxury hotels: guest profiling, high-stakes dispute resolution, VIP escort protocols, and automated cloud reservation systems.

Duration
1 Year (2 Semesters)
Eligibility
High School Diploma + Fluent English & Second Language
Core Curriculum
Opera PMS Certification, Butler Grooming, Telecommunications Protocol, Luxury Transportation Coordination, Room Assignment Logic.
Career Opportunities
Front Office Manager, Chief Concierge, Guest Relations Director, Night Auditor.
Apply for Program View Fee Structure
Advanced Certificate Interior Sciences

6. Executive Housekeeping & Facilities Hygiene

The backbone of hotel asset preservation: textile care, floral architecture, chemical safety, room inspection analytics, and eco-sustainable housekeeping operations.

Duration
1 Year (Includes Lab Practicum)
Eligibility
High School Certificate or Equivalent
Core Curriculum
Linen & Laundry Technology, Fine Material Care (Marble/Silk/Leather), Pest Management, Room Turnaround Time Optimization, Green Cleaning.
Career Opportunities
Executive Housekeeper, Director of Services, Facilities Inspector, Estate Manager.
Apply for Program View Fee Structure
Professional Diploma Global Summit Staging

7. Luxury Event, Convention & Wedding Management

Orchestrating state banquets, high-fashion galas, multi-million-dollar destination weddings, and international business conventions with creative vision and logistical precision.

Duration
2 Years (4 Semesters)
Eligibility
High School Diploma + Creative Portfolio Review
Core Curriculum
Banqueting Logistics, Stage & Light Production, Celebrity Protocol, Contract Negotiations, Risk Assessment & Crowd Safety.
Career Opportunities
Director of Events, Luxury Wedding Planner, Conference Producer, Festival Operations Manager.
